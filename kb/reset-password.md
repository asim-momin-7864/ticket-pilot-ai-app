## Reset from the login page

1. Open the Orbitly login page and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address you signed up with.
3. Open the email titled "Reset your Orbitly password" and select the link.
4. Choose a new password with at least 10 characters.

The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If it has expired, request a new one.

## If the email does not arrive

- Check your spam folder.
- Make sure you used the same email as your account.
- Wait 5 minutes, then request the link again.

## If you use SSO

Accounts that sign in with SSO do not have an Orbitly password. Reset it through your company's identity provider instead.
