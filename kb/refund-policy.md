## Who can request a refund

Annual Pro and Business subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of the first payment. Monthly plans and renewals are not eligible.

## How refunds are handled

Refund requests are reviewed by our customer-care team. Support cannot promise a refund or give a decision in chat or email. To request one, contact customer care with your workspace name and the billing email.

## Not covered

Partial refunds, credits and refunds after the 14-day window are not offered under this policy.
