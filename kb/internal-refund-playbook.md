## Eligibility check

1. Confirm the plan is annual Pro or Business.
2. Confirm the first payment was 14 days ago or less.
3. Confirm no earlier refund exists for the workspace.

## Escalation

Send every refund request to the customer-care channel with the ticket ref, workspace name and payment date. Billing approves in the billing tool. Never tell the customer a decision before billing confirms.

## Tone

Be brief and kind. Do not promise outcomes or timelines.
